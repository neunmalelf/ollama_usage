- **20260907091907**: Root cause recap — shiboken's signal connect inspects slots with CPython `PyFunction_*` APIs, which raise `SystemError` (funcobject.c:432) on Nuitka-compiled methods once extra packages are bundled (`--include-package`). That killed the menu connect and the fetch-timer reschedule (widget froze after the first fetch). All slots in `widget_qt.py` are now wrapped in a callable-instance `_Callback` — verified in the binary: menu opens, timer refreshes (Ollama connections every 30s), startup stderr clean.
